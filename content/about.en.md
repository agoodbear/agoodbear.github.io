        <div class="title">Leaving Taipei VGH for the countryside — Zhudong VH</div>
        <div class="descr">I moved to Zhudong Veterans Hospital, a district hospital at the far end of Provincial Highway 68. Medical and surgical patients came in at roughly 1:1. The hospital had plenty of resident cats we could feed, until a certain senior official decided they spread disease and had them all caught and caged. Things I remember well: a cobra showing up at my front door, the great food at the Zhudong Hakka market, and helping run a clinic at the Leshan radar station.</div>
    </li>
    <li style="--accent-color:#E24A68">
        <div class="date">2011-12</div>
        <div class="title">Leaving Zhudong for eastern Taiwan — Yuli VH</div>
        <div class="descr">I spent about 4 years in the east. We used to joke about it: great mountains, great water, great boredom. But the soil out east really does stick to you. The sky and the air there are different from anywhere else — so comfortable you want to stay forever. Step outside at night and the sky is full of stars. Yuli watermelons, Yuli noodles, Dapo Pond in Chishang, the rice straw of Fuli, Luye in Taitung. Every day off, I'd drive either south or north to go exploring and soak in the scenery. Thank you, eastern Taiwan, for four years of memories that will last a lifetime.</div>
    </li>

        <div class="date">2015-10</div>
        <div class="title">National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University Hospital</div>
        <div class="descr">So that our two kids could have an education outside the conventional school system, I left familiar surroundings once again and set off on an unfamiliar road. I bought the first home of my life — a farmhouse in the middle of the rice fields. We've been through several big typhoons there; I no longer know how to put all the ups and downs into words. All I can do is thank heaven and cherish everything I have now. As of 2024 it's been seven years. Sometimes I wonder how many more years I'll feel that emergency medicine is a road I can keep walking.</div>
